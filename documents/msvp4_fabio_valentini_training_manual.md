## 1. CORE MODEL / PHILOSOPHY (The Foundation - Never Skip This)

- **Auction Market Theory (AMT)**: Markets are continuous auctions that alternate between **balance** (efficient trading at fair value, high volume areas) and **imbalance** (one side dominant and aggressive, seeking new equilibrium). Most of the time price is in balance. Big directional moves happen during imbalance.
- **Read, Don’t Predict**: The #1 reason traders lose is trying to anticipate what the market "should" do. Only enter when the market clearly shows aggression + proper location.
- **Order Flow = Leading Information**: Price is lagging. Big order prints (bubbles), Cumulative Volume Delta (CVD), and absorption reveal real participant intent *before* price moves.
- **Volume Profile = The Map**: Shows Value Area, Point of Control (POC), and Low Volume Nodes (LVN). Always know where you are relative to volume distribution.
- **Objectivity Over Subjectivity**: Use clear filters (e.g. 20-30+ contract size) instead of subjective price action interpretation.
- **Dynamic Model vs Rigid Rules**: Market adapts daily. Treat it as a flexible model, not a cage of fixed rules.
- **Edge Decay**: Pure price action loses effectiveness when volatility changes. Order flow reads the underlying auction mechanics and adapts better.
- **Psychology Foundation**: No ego, no revenge trading. Stay out when conditions are not met. Focus on reading the auction, not P&L in the moment.

## 2. THE TWO CORE STRATEGIES

### Model 1: Trend Following / Imbalance Model (Primary - New York Session)

**Best Environment**: High volatility New York session, directional days.

**Step 1 - Market State**:
- Price must be **out of balance** (breaking away from Value Area).
- Do NOT trade in balanced/choppy conditions with this model.

**Step 2 - Location**:
- Break of structure or major swing point.
- Align with **Low Volume Node (LVN)** from the impulse profile.
- Target reference: Previous balance area or POC.

**Step 3 - Trigger (Aggression)**:
- Big aggressive orders (filtered bubbles, 20-30+ contracts on 1m/5m).
- Ideal: Pullback to LVN + strong aggression in imbalance direction.

**Entry Rules**:
- On aggression confirmation (prefer full 1-minute candle close).
- Can be aggressive (at breakout) or conservative (on retest).

**Stop Loss**:
- Tight, placed behind the aggressive order or recent swing.
- Sometimes 1-2 ticks below high to minimize slippage during acceleration.
- Rule: Be wrong immediately.

**Target**:
- Previous balance area / POC / Value Area.
- Usually take full profit (high probability of reversal at balance ~70%).

**Risk-Reward**: Typically 1:3 to 1:5. Can reach 1:10-20 on strong directional days.

**Enhancement**: Use CVD for early move to breakeven when divergence appears.

### Model 2: Mean Reversion Model (London / Compression Days)

**Best Environment**: London session, summer chop, clear consolidation.

**Market State**:
- Balanced/consolidation (price respecting Value Area highs and lows).

**Location**:
- Deep discount (below Value Area) or premium.
- Target = high volume node / POC.

**Entry**:
- Wait for **first breakout**, then retracement back into balance.
- Confirm with big order absorption.
- NEVER take the first impulsive leg.

**Stop Loss**: Very tight. Wrong = immediate exit.

**Target**: Back into balance / POC area.

**Purpose**: Complements Model 1 for smoother equity curve.

## 3. UNIVERSAL RULES, DO’S & DON’Ts

### Timing & Session Rules
- **Primary**: New York session only for Trend Model.
- Avoid first hour of London for trend following.
- Close ALL positions by end of NY session (no overnight).
- London/Compression: Use Mean Reversion Model.

### Risk Management (Non-Negotiable)
- Per trade: 0.25% – 0.5% of account (up to 1% only after daily profit buffer).
- Max daily risk: ~2% (equivalent to 8-11 small stops).
- Build position size using daily profit ("house money").
- Move to breakeven aggressively on confirmation.
- Scale out on weakness or at targets.
- High trade count = fast drawdown recovery.

### Execution & Trade Management
- Require full 1m candle confirmation to filter fakeouts.
- Filter big orders (20-30+ contracts).
- Use profile drawn on impulse swings for accurate LVN.
- Absorption (heavy volume with no price follow-through) = strong squeeze potential.
- Trail/adjust stops only when new aggression supports it.
- In strong trends: Selective trailing.
- In chop: Reduce size dramatically or stay out.

### DO’s
- Build profit consistently throughout the day.
- Adapt your narrative instantly when key levels break.
- Use both models together for balanced equity curve.
- Journal every trade with screenshots.
- Stay extremely patient in compression.
- Scale in on strong continuation (using profit only).

### DON’Ts
- Do not predict or anticipate moves.
- Do not revenge trade or force setups.
- Do not hold positions overnight.
- Do not take the first impulsive move in reversion setups.
- Do not marry your bias — change when market proves you wrong.
- Do not exceed daily risk limit.
- Do not ignore session-specific behavior.
- Do not use wide stops hoping for reversal.

### When to Stay Completely Out
- Pure compression without clear imbalance.
- No aggression + no LVN alignment.
- Pre-market / pre-NY open noise.
- After hitting daily risk limit.
- Unclear auction direction.
