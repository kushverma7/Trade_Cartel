1. Opening entry fires at 9:50 / 9:55 / 10:00 AEST — normal trail system
2. When SL is hit during session (9:00-12:00 AEST): instead of closing flat,
   immediately reverse to opposite direction at the SL price
3. Flip SL = original SL x 1.5 (wider stop on reversal trade)
4. Flip also trails 30pts behind peak — captures the reversal move
5. Max 1 flip per day (configurable) to prevent over-trading
WHEN IT WORKS BEST

RISK NOTE
