Strategy Implementation Details
NQ Master CLC System
CLC Pillars (each = 1 point, max 3):
Pillar 1 — Context: Supertrend (14, 3.0) direction. Bull = price above ST line; Bear = price
below.
Pillar 2 — Location: Liquidity sweep of 20-bar high/low OR price within 0.5×ATR of VWAP
±1SD/2SD bands.
Pillar 3 — Confirmation: CVD rising/falling (14-bar EMA smoothed) + aggression bubble
(2× avg volume + strong body candle) OR delta outlier (2.5× avg delta on single bar).
Signal grades: A-grade (3⁄3) = full size (1% risk). B-grade (2⁄3, location + confirm only) = half
size (0.5% risk).
Stop loss: Swept level ± 0.1×ATR buffer. Fallback: signal bar high/low ± 0.1×ATR.
Take profit (3-tier):
TP1 → VWAP midline: close 40%, move SL to BE
TP2 → VWAP 2SD band: close 40% of original
TP3 → VWAP 3SD band: close all remaining
CVD divergence rule: If price up but CVD falling (or vice versa), SL moves to BE immediately.
3-loss rule: After 3 losses at the same price level (±5 pts), that level is skipped for the
remainder of the day.
Daily stop: −2R cumulative → no more trades that session.
Crypto Scalp Model v1
Session phases (per NY and London session):
Q1 (bars 0–5): Accumulation — mark range high/low, no action.
Q2 (bars 6–11): Manipulation — sweep must occur here. Bull sweep: wick ≥0.25×ATR below
Q1 low, close back above. Bear sweep: wick ≥0.25×ATR above Q1 high, close back below.
Q3 (bars 12–17): Distribution — entry window only.
Long entry (all required): In Q3 + Q1 low swept in Q2 + displacement candle (bull body
≥0.6×ATR) + MSS (breaks above 5-bar swing high) + close above VWAP + close below
midnight open (00:00 EST).
Short entry (all required): In Q3 + Q1 high swept in Q2 + displacement candle (bear body
≥0.6×ATR) + MSS (breaks below 5-bar swing low) + close below VWAP + close above midnight
open.
Stop loss: Swept Q1 level ± 0.1×ATR buffer.
Take profit:
TP1 → 1R: close 60%, move SL to BE
Runner (40%): hold with SL at BE, hard max at VWAP 2SD band
Daily stop: −2R cumulative → session stopped.

disabled or used only in very high-confluence scenarios.

Limitations & Disclaimers
1. No look-ahead bias was introduced — all signals are generated on bar close and entries
are taken on the next bar’s open.
2. CVD is approximated using volume × (2 × buy fraction − 1) since tick-level data is
unavailable from Yahoo Finance. Real CVD from a DOM feed would be more accurate.
3. Aggression bubbles are approximated using volume spikes + body size; actual order flow
aggression requires Level 2 data.
4. Zero Print zones (Carmine’s volume voids) are not implemented as a location signal due to
data limitations. Their inclusion would likely increase A-grade signal frequency.
5. NQ futures were backtested on the continuous front-month contract (NQ=F). Roll
adjustments are handled by Yahoo Finance’s auto-adjust.
6. BTC-USD spot was used as a proxy for BTCUSDT perpetual futures. Funding rates and
perp-specific dynamics are not modelled.
7. Past performance does not guarantee future results.
