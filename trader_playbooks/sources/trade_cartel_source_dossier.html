Standing rules governing all of the above

  • Never discard. Low-credibility, contradictory, or superseded material stays in the register, clearly tagged — it may connect to something later.
  • One brain per voice. New material from an already-processed author extends their existing file in place; it never forks a new engine.
  • Confluence, not standalone output. The 21 voices above are applied as confluence inside strategies — delivered as a knowledge layer, not as 21 separate products, unless directly asked for.
  • No fabricated proof. As of 2026-07-20, the project's original PF 3.656 backtest was formally retracted for being too small a sample (~2 months, 117 trades) to call evidence — nothing in this repo earns "proven" or "flagship" language without a real backtest on a real sample.
