Result: The chart shows only a single colored line. Green = bullish bias. Red = bearish bias. This line is the ENTRY SIGNAL generator.
Signal Logic
CRITICAL: Always act on candle CLOSE, not mid-candle color. Wait for the 5-minute candle to fully close before entering or exiting.
Key Difference vs SuperTrend
Half Trend does NOT flip on price touching the trend line. It stays directional longer than SuperTrend — meaning it holds winning trades through pullbacks and avoids early exits. This is the core mechanical advantage over SuperTrend-based systems.
2.2 Indicator 2 — Directional Movement Index (DMI)
Built-in TradingView indicator. Search "Directional Movement Index" in the indicator library.
Input Settings
Style Settings
The 23 Level — Trading Zone Gate
Draw a horizontal line at ADX value 23 on the DMI panel. This line is the "trading zone gate":
ADX (black) ABOVE 23 → Trading is active. Signals are valid.
ADX (black) BELOW 23 → No trading zone. All signals are IGNORED regardless of Half Trend color.
This filter eliminates low-volatility chop and flat market conditions where trend-following strategies fail.
DMI Filter Logic (Directional Bias)
The +DI (green) and -DI (red) lines are compared against EACH OTHER — not against the ADX (black) or the 23 level:
You cannot take a long trade when -DI is above +DI, even if Half Trend turns green. You cannot take a short trade when +DI is above -DI, even if Half Trend turns red.
3. FULL ENTRY & EXIT RULES
3.1 Long (Buy) Entry — All 3 Conditions Required
If all 3 conditions are met simultaneously → ENTER LONG at candle close.
3.2 Short (Sell) Entry — All 3 Conditions Required
If all 3 conditions are met simultaneously → ENTER SHORT at candle close.
3.3 Exit Rules
LONG EXIT — Close long position when:
Half Trend turns RED on a 5m candle close
OR: ADX (black) drops below 23 (no-trade zone re-entered)
SHORT EXIT — Close short position when:
Half Trend turns GREEN on a 5m candle close
OR: ADX (black) drops below 23
NEVER exit mid-candle. Always wait for the full 5-minute candle to close before confirming a signal change.
3.4 Re-Entry Rules
After a stop loss is hit:
Do NOT re-enter against the DMI directional bias (e.g., do not go long if -DI is still above +DI)
Wait for a new valid signal: all 3 conditions met simultaneously on a candle close
If 2 stop losses hit in the same day: STOP TRADING for that day. No more trades regardless of signals.
4. RISK MANAGEMENT & POSITION SIZING
4.1 Risk/Reward Philosophy
This strategy is NOT win-rate-dependent. The edge comes from win size. The developer demonstrated this math explicitly:
The developer explicitly observed 1:3, 1:4, and 1:6 risk/reward ratios in BANK NIFTY and INFOSYS examples. Even with win rates below 50%, the system can be profitable if RR ratios hold.
4.2 Stop Loss Placement
Initial stop loss: Below the entry candle low (for longs) / above the entry candle high (for shorts)
No fixed pip/point stop — stop is defined by the Half Trend signal reversal
If Half Trend flips to opposite color → that IS the stop loss trigger
Developer noted stops are "very small" by design — the Half Trend indicator structure keeps stops tight
4.3 Daily Loss Limit
Maximum 2 stop losses per day
If 2 SLs are hit in the same trading session: CLOSE ALL positions and DO NOT take any further trades that day
This protects against revenge trading and high-volatility chop days
4.4 Position Sizing
Developer referenced "position sizing" and "risk management" but did not give a specific % per trade in this video
For options: Use ITM (in-the-money) strikes — buy the nearest ITM strike to the current market price
For expiry day only: Move 50–100 points further OTM than the nearest ITM strike to reduce theta risk
For futures/equity: Normal position sizing applies; 0.25–0.5% account risk per trade is a reasonable starting point
5. OPTIONS STRIKE PRICE SELECTION
5.1 Standard Days (Non-Expiry)
Buy the nearest ITM (In-The-Money) strike to the current market price:
For CALLS (long signal): Buy the strike just BELOW the current market price (e.g., market at 16,540 → buy 16,500 CE)
For PUTS (short signal): Buy the strike just ABOVE the current market price (e.g., market at 16,540 → buy 16,550 PE)
Rationale: ITM options have higher delta (move more in line with the underlying), lower time value loss per tick, and better liquidity in most Indian index options.
5.2 Expiry Day Adjustment
On the weekly/monthly expiry day, theta decay accelerates rapidly for ITM/ATM options
For calls: Go 50–100 strike points DEEPER ITM (lower strike) than normal
For puts: Go 50–100 strike points DEEPER ITM (higher strike) than normal
This reduces gamma risk and the impact of rapid time decay on expiry

7.2 Calculations
Half Trend
Replicate the "everget" Half Trend logic:
Uses ATR-based channel to determine trend direction
Plots a single line that is colored green (bullish) or red (bearish)
Does NOT flip on intra-candle price movement — only on confirmed candle close
Key difference from SuperTrend: uses channel-based confirmation rather than price-touching-line flip
DMI / ADX on 15m
Use request.security() in Pine Script to read DMI values from 15-minute timeframe:
adx_val = request.security(syminfo.tickerid, "15", ta.dmi(adx_len, adx_smoothing)[0])
plusDI = request.security(syminfo.tickerid, "15", ta.dmi(adx_len, adx_smoothing)[1])
minusDI = request.security(syminfo.tickerid, "15", ta.dmi(adx_len, adx_smoothing)[2])
Draw ADX value on DMI panel, draw horizontal line at adx_threshold (23)

9. DEVELOPER NOTES, WARNINGS & EDGE CASES
9.1 What Developer Explicitly Warned
Win rate is NOT high — expect 50–65% range. Do not expect 70%+ win rate.
Some days will have 2 consecutive stop losses — accept this and stop trading that day.
Strategy will sometimes give signals in sideways/choppy markets and lose. ADX > 23 reduces but does not eliminate this.
Do not take BTST if you are an intraday trader — know your trading style before entering
Expiry day has higher risk due to theta decay — adjust strike selection accordingly.
9.2 Where the Strategy is Strongest
Trending days with clear momentum — Half Trend stays one color for long periods.
Gap-up or gap-down opens following a BTST signal — massive RR outcomes.
Liquid instruments: BANK NIFTY, NIFTY, major large-cap stocks (INFY, GRASIM, AIRTEL referenced).
Instruments where ATR is high relative to spread — ensures stop losses are clean.
9.3 Where the Strategy Struggles
Sideways/range-bound markets — ADX will be below 23, blocking entries (this is by design).
First-hour high-volatility opens — can trigger large initial stop losses (developer showed 2% SL in first candle of GRASIM).
Small-cap stocks with low liquidity — spread impact on entry/exit quality.
Expiry day afternoon — gamma squeeze and rapid IV changes can override technical signals.
9.4 Indicator Choice Rationale
Half Trend vs SuperTrend — explicitly contrasted by developer:
SuperTrend flips when PRICE TOUCHES the trend line.
Half Trend uses ATR-based channel confirmation — requires price to establish beyond the channel before flipping.
Result: Half Trend "holds winners through pullbacks" — does not whipsaw out of valid trends on minor retracements.
This is the mechanical reason for superior RR ratios — exits come later, captures more of the move.
9.5 DMI Period Choice (13)
Developer chose 13 as both ADX smoothing and DI length.
Rationale: "Fibonacci number" — 13 is in the Fibonacci sequence, commonly used in technical analysis.
Developer stated this was personally backtested, not taken from another source.
Standard default for DMI is usually 14 — this is a deliberate deviation.

11. POTENTIAL ENHANCEMENTS FOR YOUR BUILD
The following improvements are NOT in the original strategy but are logical extensions based on the underlying indicator logic and your existing trading stack:
ADX slope filter: Only take entries when ADX is rising (not just above 23 but trending up) — removes entries at peak trend momentum.
ATR-based stop loss: Instead of relying solely on Half Trend flip for exit, calculate entry ATR and set hard stop at 1.0x–1.5x ATR below/above entry. This defines initial SL size precisely.
Volume filter: Add minimum volume condition (e.g., bar volume > 20-period average volume) to confirm institutional participation at entry.
Session filter: Restrict entries to high-liquidity windows — 09:20–11:30 AM and 01:30–03:00 PM IST. Avoid the 12–1 PM dead zone.
Multi-stock scanner: Alert fires across a watchlist of 5–10 stocks simultaneously. Trade the first valid signal each day.
BTST detection: Auto-detect when a signal fires after 2:30 PM — flag as BTST rather than intraday. Send separate alert.
Trailing stop after 1:2 RR hit: If trade moves 2x the initial SL distance in your favor, move stop to breakeven and trail from there.
