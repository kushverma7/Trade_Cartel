WHY THE OPENING TRADE WORKS
Gap & Commit
ASX 200 gaps on open based on overnight
US/EU futures — price must commit to direction.
First Candle Authority
The 10:00 AM 5-min bar establishes the daily
bias that institutions defend for 10–20pts.
Institutional Order Flow
DE v4 signals align with large institutional
orders placed at the ASX electronic open.
Tight Spread at Open
AU200 spread is tightest at open (~1pt),
ensuring accurate fills and reduced slippage.
Directional Momentum
Markets commit to direction in the first
10–20pts before mean-reversion kicks in.
