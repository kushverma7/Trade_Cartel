8. HOW TO SAVE PROFITS — SPECIFIC CODE FIXES
Fix 1: Strong-trend TP1 gate (CRITICAL)
Disable TP1 when the trade is already running well. Trending days go 30-50pts.
_tp1_allowed = (_dir==1 and (_best-_ep) < 15.0) or (_dir==-1 and (_ep-_best) < 15.0)
_tp1_long_trigger = _dir==1 and not _tp1Done and _tp1_allowed and _long_in_profit and close<=e8
_tp1_short_trigger = _dir==-1 and not _tp1Done and _tp1_allowed and _short_in_profit and close>=e8
Fix 2: Raise flip multiplier to 2.0 (+$1.77M)
Wider flip SL = bigger immediate take-profit on the flip. Proven in sweep.
flip_sl_mult = input.float(2.0, 'Flip SL multiplier', ...)
Fix 3: Raise SL to 20pts (+$1.34M)
Tighter SL clips too many valid setups before the flip can fire.

Fix 4: Raise trail trigger to 5pts
3pt trigger fires on normal 5m noise. 5pts waits for real directional move.
trail_trig = input.float(5.0, 'Trail trigger (pts)', ...)
Fix 5: Late-session entry cutoff at 11:30
