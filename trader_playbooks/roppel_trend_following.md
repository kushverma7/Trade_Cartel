## Risk architecture (portable to our strategies)
- 3-5-7 SCALED STOP: exit 1/3 at -3%, 1/3 at -5%, 1/3 at -7% from
  entry (adapted for gold scalps: thirds at 0.5R/0.75R/1R of the stop
  distance). Never carry full size to the full stop.
- Hard cap: max 0.5% of portfolio risk per position (with size, real
  slippage forces scaling out early).
- CUSHION PROTOCOL: no YTD cushion = tight and risk-averse; big
  cushion = play against it. [2nd independent source after Valentini]
- Drawdown control IS the compounding mechanism; calendar line in the
  sand — "don't give it back" trigger on big up-years.
- Position size is the ultimate safety: sized right, even a botched
  exit survives. "Always stay in the game."

## Entry/exit doctrine
- NEVER buy weakness; buy strength (RS leaders breaking out first).
- Real breakout = "tectonic tsunami of volume". [Corroborates H9
  DRIVE-vs-THIN — first independent support]
- Sell into climax tops. Stocks top technically before fundamentals.
- SHAKEOUT + 3: stopped out but the setup immediately rebuilds ->
  re-enter (expectation breaker). Cousin of our reclaim family.
- Round numbers = magnets/failure points ("Livermore large numbers").
  [2nd source after Valentini]
- Market regime gate: MAs aligned -> accelerate; ambivalent or
  deteriorating -> back off. Bear markets BUILD the next setups
  (cup-and-handles only form in corrections).

## Psychology / craft
- Overtrading + not cutting losses = the rookie killers; selectivity
  ("don't swing at every pitch").
- The big money is in the sitting (position context).
- Mental fitness affects drawdown control — trade smaller when not A+.
- New traders: don't run; spend the early years learning cheaply so
  the first real capital doesn't die to rookie mistakes.
