7. VERDICT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The 10:00 candle's BODY carries no usable information. The permutation
  null reproduces the headline result — same selected setup, same
  expectancy, same t — from data where the candle has been scrambled.

  What is real is narrower and more useful: displacement from the 10:00
  open, traded with momentum and a target well above the stop, beats
  random entry decisively (z ≈ 5.8). The candle body is riding that; it is
  not creating it.

  For a dependable, smooth equity curve this is NOT there yet. One year is
  ~250 setups, the walk-forward swings from -3.86 to +6.25 points a trade
  fold to fold, and only 13% of the long-leg parameter grid is positive in
  both halves of the sample. A smooth in-sample curve selected from 1,456
  candidates is the expected output of the search, not evidence against it.
