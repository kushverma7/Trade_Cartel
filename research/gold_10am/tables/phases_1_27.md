## Phase 27 — Realistic execution cost

The Pine `costPts` mechanism shrinks the SL and TP distances by half the cost. That is not transaction-cost accounting: it changes where the orders sit rather than charging the fill. Here the round-trip cost is **charged to the P&L** and the SL/TP stay where the rule puts them.

**Measured Dukascopy XAU/USD spread** across the 339 setup days, 10:00–16:00 Melbourne: median **$0.630**, p90 $0.860, p99 $1.660 (n=185,601 minute observations). A round trip crosses it twice, so the spread alone costs about **$1.26** per trade before any slippage.
